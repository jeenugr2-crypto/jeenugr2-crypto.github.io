About Pragyan Academy

Maths, taught with patience and play.

Pragyan Academy is a personal maths tutoring practice built on a simple belief — every child can love maths once they're taught the way their mind works. We slow down on the concepts that matter, speed up on the ones kids find easy, and turn every lesson into a small discovery.

Parents tell us the difference shows up everywhere — in school scores, in homework time at home, and most of all in the way their child talks about maths at the dinner table.
